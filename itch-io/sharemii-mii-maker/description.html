Create Nintendo-style Miis right in your browser — no download, no account required.

ShareMii Mii Maker is a free online character creator powered by ShareMii.net, the community plaza for browsing, sharing, and collecting Mii QR codes. Design a Mii from scratch (or randomize for inspiration), then export a QR code you can scan on your console.

What you can do

  • Customize everything — face shape, hair, eyes, nose, mouth, glasses, facial hair, height & build
  • Randomize — roll the dice and tweak from there
  • Save a QR code — download a PNG to scan on supported Nintendo hardware
  • Share on ShareMii Plaza — optional one-click path to upload your Mii to the community gallery at ShareMii.net

Supported consoles (QR export)

QR codes work with Mii QR scanning on:

  • Nintendo 3DS
  • Wii U
  • Tomodachi Life

How to play

  1. Click Launch to open the Mii Maker.
  2. Pick a category (Face, Hair, Eyes, etc.) and adjust your Mii.
  3. Give your Mii a name, then click Finish.
  4. Save QR code to download the image for your console.
  5. Want to share? Click Upload to ShareMii Plaza to open ShareMii.net and publish your Mii to the community.

About ShareMii.net

ShareMii.net is a fan-made community hub for Mii QR codes — browse a public plaza, yeah your favorites, follow creators, remix Miis, and build collections. Think of it as the plaza the community never got after Miiverse.
